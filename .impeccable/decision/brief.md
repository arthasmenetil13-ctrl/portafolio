## Scope

Home page of Carlos Llanos's portfolio (`/` Spanish, `/en` English). Visitor mode: Experience. The work leads from the first viewport.

## Audience and job

Remote recruiters and tech leads arriving from LinkedIn, mostly on a phone. Job: understand in one scroll that he ships and operates a real product, then contact him (email, LinkedIn, GitHub) or open the project.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is the hand-painted signboard of a Sincelejo business that opens every day: a yellow enamel board whose "HOY SE VIGILA" placards change as Estados Judiciales finds new court actions. It refuses the dark dev-portfolio hero (name, tagline, gradient, card row) and the white résumé grid.

OWN-WORLD: Committed colour. Sign-yellow enamel ground (#F6C419 family) owns most of the page; red (#D7261E) for filete borders and the painted drop shadows of block caps; cobalt (#1D3F9A) for secondary boards; cream placards (#F4ECD8) with ink (#14110F) lettering. Block-cap signage display face with a hard offset shadow, a sturdy grotesque for reading text. Components are tin plaques: cream or cobalt boards with a double painted border, screw dots at the corners, hung placards with slot-in lettering. No glass, no gradients, no neon.

STORY: The visitor sees the system working (a court case being watched, a new action arriving, the alert that goes out), understands that one person built and runs it, reads the stack and history as honest measures, and leaves with an email, LinkedIn or GitHub click.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top band: a painted sign strip with "CARLOS LLANOS" in large block caps, "Desarrollador full stack · Sincelejo, Colombia" under it, ES/EN switch at the right as a small plaque. Centre-left, 60% width: the "HOY SE VIGILA" board (cream plaque) with the real Bogotá date and three or four placard rows (radicado, juzgado, estado), one of which flips to "ACTUACIÓN NUEVA" every few seconds. Centre-right: the alert plaque showing the message that goes out by WhatsApp and email for that row, labelled "Datos de ejemplo". Bottom of viewport: a row of painted action plaques: "Ver el proyecto", "Escríbeme", LinkedIn, GitHub. Primary action "Escríbeme" (mailto) is the red plaque.

FORM: Rótulo pintado de la Costa, position 3 on the ordered grounded list (1 lista de estados del juzgado, 2 cuaderno cuadriculado de ingeniería, 3 rótulo pintado, 4 trenza del sombrero vueltiao, 5 telegrama certificado, 6 notificación de celular, 7 plano de ingeniería). seed:4c58cd4580e22c2e. Signature interaction: the placard flip on the HOY board synchronized with the alert plaque re-lettering; second interaction: the stack swatch row highlights the parts of the Estados Judiciales architecture board each technology powers. Motion grammar: tin placards swing and settle (short rotate + settle), nothing floats or fades in on scroll; everything honours prefers-reduced-motion.
Raises: character sheet (swatch governs regions), j-card (real dates and durations right-aligned), cyclorama (states named and patterned, never colour alone), minihompy (board is a living log; new project = one data entry), cityscape (real Bogotá date and hour).
